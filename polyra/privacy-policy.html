Privacy Policy — Polyra: Neon Survival

Last updated: November 5, 2026

This policy describes how the mobile application Polyra: Neon Survival ("the App"), developed by Arkan Games ("we"), handles information when you use it. The App is a single-player game. It does not require creating an account with us, and we do not ask for your name, email, phone number, or location. Signing in to Google Play Games is optional and only used for the global leaderboard.

1. Information collected

The App does not collect information that directly identifies you. The following information is processed anonymously or pseudonymously:

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App shows rewarded ads through Google AdMob. They are optional: an ad only plays if you choose to watch one, for example to continue a run after losing or to get new upgrade choices. To serve and measure ads, AdMob may process the device Advertising ID, IP address, and ad interaction data. Google acts as the advertising provider; its processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy and the AdMob policies (see also How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services). If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the App will ask for your consent through Google's consent form before showing personalized ads, and you can change your choice at any time with the AD PRIVACY button on the App's main screen.

Leaderboard (Google Play Games Services)

If you are signed in to Google Play Games on your device, the App sends your best sector reached to Google Play Games Services to rank it on the global leaderboard. The leaderboard shows your score with your Play Games profile (gamer name and avatar), and other players can see it. Sign-in and your profile are managed by Google, not by us; their processing is governed by the Google Privacy Policy. You can play without signing in: your score simply stays on your device.

Usage analytics (PostHog)

We use PostHog to understand how the App is played and to improve it. Gameplay events are recorded (for example: game session start, sector reached, upgrades chosen, ads watched, skins bought) along with technical device data such as device model, operating system version, screen size and App version, and an anonymous identifier generated by the App itself. Error reports are also sent automatically to help us fix crashes. PostHog receives your device's IP address when events are sent and may use it to estimate your approximate location. We do not collect your name or contact details. This data is used in aggregate for statistics and diagnostics. You can turn it off at any time with the SHARE USAGE DATA switch in the App's Settings.

Data stored on your device

Your game progress, such as your best sector reached and your gems (in-game currency earned by playing), is stored locally on your device. It is not uploaded to any server of ours and is removed if you uninstall the App. The only exception is your best sector, which is sent to the Google Play Games leaderboard if you are signed in.

2. How it is used

This information is used only to run the App, display ads and give you the rewards you chose to watch them for, measure ad delivery, show the global leaderboard, and understand how the App is played so we can improve it and fix errors. We do not sell your data.

3. Who it is shared with

Information is shared only with the providers needed to deliver these services: Google AdMob (advertising), Google Play Games Services (leaderboard) and PostHog (analytics). We do not share information with other third parties for commercial purposes.

4. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us so we can delete it.

5. Your choices

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID from your Android device settings (Settings → Google → Ads). If the App asked for your ad consent, you can change it with the AD PRIVACY button on the App's main screen. You can sign out of Google Play Games, or delete your Play Games profile and its data, from your Google Play Games settings. You can turn off usage analytics with the SHARE USAGE DATA switch in the App's Settings. You can delete all local data by uninstalling the App.

6. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally. The "Last updated" date reflects the current version.
